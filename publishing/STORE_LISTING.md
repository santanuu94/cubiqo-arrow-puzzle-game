## Game Description
**CUBIQO: Arrow Escape** is a crisp, colourful directional logic puzzle game with 100 handcrafted levels and a fresh Daily Challenge every day.

Each cubical tile on the board points in one of four directions—up, right, down, or left. An arrow can only escape when its forward exit path to the edge of the board is completely clear of other blocks. Observe the dependencies, plan your sequence carefully, and clear the entire grid!

### Key Features
- **100 Progressive Levels:** Ramp smoothly from 3×3 learning boards to intricate 7×7 master puzzles.
- **3-Heart Lifeline Challenge:** Every blocked tap costs one heart—aim for a flawless 3-star solve without losing a single life!
- **Daily Challenge & Streaks:** Play the same deterministic puzzle as everyone else each day and build your streak.
- **Unlockable Themes & Achievements:** Earn coins, collect 13 achievements, and unlock 6 additional visual themes (*Ocean Breeze*, *Sunset Neon*, *Emerald Forest*, *Midnight Cyber*, *Royal Gold*, and *Daily Aurora*) at milestone levels.
- **Smart Hints:** Stuck on a tricky chain? Use your free per-level hint to highlight a guaranteed-clear arrow.

## How to Play & Controls
- **Objective:** Clear every directional arrow block from the board.
- **Rule:** Tap or click an arrow only if no other active arrow sits in front of it along its pointing direction.
- **3-Heart System:** You have 3 hearts per level. Tapping a blocked arrow costs 1 heart. Losing all 3 hearts ends the attempt—replay freely or restore a heart using earned coins.
- **Desktop Controls:**
  - **Left Click:** Select an arrow to slide it off the board
  - **H:** Use a Hint
  - **R:** Restart current level
  - **Esc:** Pause / Resume
- **Mobile Controls:**
  - **Tap:** Select an arrow or menu button
